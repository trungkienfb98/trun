# Publishing and scheduling

## Connection and authentication

Use a platform connector or API first when it fully supports the action. Otherwise select a supported browser according to the Browser skill. Open Facebook or Meta Business Suite. If authentication is required, ask the user to sign in directly in that browser and resume after confirmation. Never inspect cookies or accept credentials in chat.

## Prepare the Reel

1. Verify visible page name and destination URL.
2. Open the Reel composer.
3. Upload the approved MP4.
4. Paste the full approved caption.
5. Confirm the content type is Reel/Thước phim.
6. Keep promotions, boosts, ads, and paid options off unless explicitly requested.
7. Review platform warnings and processing state.

## Choose the next slot

Read configured times, daily limit, timezone, and publishing ledger. Check the live platform schedule when available because it is authoritative. Use the earliest future free configured slot. If a day is full, advance to the next day. Never schedule two posts for the same page at one timestamp.

## Final approval

Before the irreversible click, show page name, video name, caption opening, date/time, timezone, and mode. Require explicit approval such as “Duyệt”, “Đăng”, or “Lên lịch”. An older approval for another video does not authorize this one.

After clicking, verify confirmation or the scheduled entry and log its identifier. If success cannot be verified, report uncertainty and do not create a duplicate attempt automatically.
